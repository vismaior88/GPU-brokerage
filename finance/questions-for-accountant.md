# Questions for the accountants (US and Montenegro)
_Last checked: 2026-11-09_

## Summary

- You need **two** short engagements: a US preparer who files Form 5472 for foreign-owned LLCs, and a Montenegrin accountant (računovođa / poreski savjetnik) for one consultation on how Montenegro treats the LLC.
- **Do the Montenegrin consultation first.** Its answer decides whether keeping the LLC is even sensible (see `finance/tax-notes.md`).
- Published prices for Form 5472 + pro-forma 1120 range from about **$299–300 flat** at low-cost services to **$750–1,500** at CPA firms; late-year clean-ups cost more [1][3][4].
- Montenegrin bookkeeping is quoted by vendors at roughly **EUR 100–250/month** for a small company [5]; a one-off consultation should cost much less, but I found no published price **(unverified)**.
- A realistic tax/compliance budget inside the $2,000 limit: about $300–750 for the US filing, one paid Montenegrin consultation, plus the state fee and registered agent. That is likely $600–1,300 of the $2,000 — a large share. Ask for fixed quotes in writing.
- Bring to both: LLC formation documents, EIN letter, state of formation, date formed, list of every transfer between you and the LLC, and whether Form 5472 was filed for each past year.

## Details

### A. US accountant (non-resident LLC specialist)

| # | Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | My LLC was formed on [date] in [state]. Was Form 5472 with a pro-forma 1120 required for each past year, and was it filed? If not, how do we file late with a reasonable-cause statement, and what does it cost? | The penalty is $25,000 per missed or incomplete filing. Fixing it before an IRS notice is far cheaper [6] |
| 2 | Which of my transactions are "reportable" (capital I put in, expenses I paid personally, distributions to me)? Do I file in a year with no such transactions? | Decides whether you must file every year; almost any owner–LLC money movement counts [6] |
| 3 | What are the exact due date and extension date for my LLC, and will you fax-file and give me the confirmation sheet? | These forms cannot be e-filed; proof of timely filing is your only defence [6] |
| 4 | I do all work from Montenegro, have no US office, staff or agents. Please confirm in writing that my referral/brokerage commissions are foreign-source and not effectively connected income, so I owe no US income tax and file no Form 1040-NR. | This is the basis of the whole "no US tax" position. There is no US–Montenegro treaty to fall back on [7] |
| 5 | What would change that answer: trips to the US where I negotiate, a US-based sub-agent on commission, a US co-working address, a US employee? | Lets you avoid accidentally creating a US trade or business |
| 6 | US suppliers will ask for a W-8. Is Form W-8BEN in my own name (LLC on line 7) correct? What do I say if a supplier demands a W-9, W-8BEN-E or W-8ECI, or wants to withhold 30%? | The wrong form can trigger 30% withholding or a wrong statement to the IRS [8] |
| 7 | Will suppliers report my commissions on Form 1042-S or 1099, and does that create any filing for me? | Avoids IRS mismatch letters |
| 8 | What are my state obligations in [state] (annual report/tax, registered agent, any state income, gross-receipts or sales tax on brokerage services)? | Delaware charges $400/year due 1 June; other states differ [9] |
| 9 | Does US sales tax apply to brokerage/referral fees charged to US business customers in any state where I might exceed a nexus threshold? | Usually no for B2B services, but not verified state by state |
| 10 | Please confirm no FinCEN BOI report is due for a US-formed LLC after the August 2026 final rule, and tell me if that changes. | Rule exempts domestic companies; the statute remains [10] |
| 11 | Should the LLC stay disregarded, or is there any reason to elect corporate treatment? | Electing corporation status creates US corporate tax; almost certainly "stay disregarded", but confirm |
| 12 | What bookkeeping do you need from me each year, and in what format? | Keeps the annual fee at the low end |
| 13 | If I later close the LLC or move the business to another company, what final filings are needed? | A final Form 5472 is required; needed for the Q29 decision |

### B. Montenegrin accountant / tax adviser

| # | Question | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | I am a Montenegrin tax resident and sole owner of a US LLC that the US treats as transparent. How does the Tax Administration classify it: a foreign company, or my own business activity? | Decides which tax applies and what must be registered |
| 2 | I manage the LLC alone from Montenegro. Is it a Montenegrin tax resident through place of effective management, or does it have a permanent establishment here? If yes, what must be registered and filed? | A foreign company managed from Montenegro can be taxed here at 9–15% corporate tax [11][12] |
| 3 | If instead the income is mine personally: is it self-employment income (0/9/15%), must I register as a preduzetnik, and what social contributions are due? Is lump-sum (paušal) taxation available below EUR 30,000 turnover? | Could be the cheapest lawful route at low income [13][14] |
| 4 | How are distributions from the LLC to my personal account taxed (15% as dividends? plus municipal surtax?), and when do I declare them? | Avoids undeclared foreign income [13][14] |
| 5 | Since I pay no US income tax and there is no treaty, is there anything to credit, and is there any double-tax risk? | Confirms the full Montenegrin rate applies |
| 6 | VAT: does the LLC's turnover count toward the EUR 30,000 registration threshold if the business is run from here? For B2B services to foreign companies, is the place of supply abroad? What changes for a Montenegrin customer? | Registration cannot be undone for three years [15] |
| 7 | Do I have any duty to report the foreign company or its US bank account to the Tax Administration or Central Bank? | Hidden foreign accounts are a common penalty trigger |
| 8 | Compare for me, at EUR 10k, 30k and 100k annual profit: (a) keep the US LLC, (b) register as preduzetnik, (c) open a d.o.o. Include tax, contributions and your annual fees. | Gives a numbers-based answer to Q29 |
| 9 | For a d.o.o. with a sole founder-director: is a minimum salary with contributions mandatory? What are your monthly fees? | Vendor sites claim a mandatory minimum salary; unverified [5] |
| 10 | Does my residence permit status (if applicable) depend on having a local company or paying a minimum amount of tax? | Some vendor sites mention a minimum annual tax for permit renewal; unverified [5] |
| 11 | What records must I keep in Montenegro, in which language, and for how long? | Sets up bookkeeping correctly from day one |
| 12 | Are law changes expected in 2027 (EU-alignment, new VAT law, CFC rules) that would change your answers? | Montenegro is aligning with EU rules; a new VAT law is tied to accession [16] |

How to choose on a small budget:
- Ask each US provider: "Is the price fixed? Does it include Form 7004 if needed, fax filing, and the confirmation page? Who signs as preparer?"
- For Montenegro, ask for the answer **in writing**, ideally with references to the law, so you can show it later if questioned.
- Avoid formation agents who sell "tax-free US LLC" packages and also do the tax advice; they have an interest in telling you there is nothing to pay.
