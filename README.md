# GPU compute brokerage

Working project for a one-person GPU compute brokerage run through a US LLC by a founder living in Montenegro. It holds the research, decisions, compliance rules, contract outlines and trackers for the business.

## Current status
_Updated: 2026-11-09 (kickoff session)_

- **Stage:** research complete, nothing launched. No supplier or buyer has been contacted. No money has been spent.
- **Proposed direction:** referral partner paid by disclosed supplier commission; niche of 8–128 GPU deals for European and Western Balkans buyers wanting EU-hosted capacity. See `business-plan.md`.
- **Waiting on the founder:** decisions 5–13 in `decisions/decision-log.md` and questions F1–F7 in `open-questions.md`.
- **Most urgent:** check past Form 5472 filings for the LLC; get Montenegrin tax advice before first revenue; decide on DSC Europe (Belgrade, 23–27 November).
- **Next rule check:** 2026-12-01 (export controls and sanctions change often).

## Reading the research
- Every research file was checked on the date at its top. Prices, programs and rules change fast; re-check before relying on them.
- Items marked **(unverified)** could not be confirmed from a source and must not be treated as fact.
- Legal, tax and compliance content is research, not professional advice. The questions for professionals are in `legal/questions-for-lawyer.md` and `finance/questions-for-accountant.md`.

## Working rules
- Verify before stating; cite sources.
- Compliance is non-negotiable; no workarounds for export controls, sanctions or KYC.
- Nothing that costs money, commits the founder or contacts a third party happens without the founder's approval.
- End each working session by updating this status section, the decision log and the open questions.
