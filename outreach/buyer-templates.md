# Buyer outreach templates
_Last checked: 2026-11-09_

Drafts for the founder to edit and send personally. Nothing has been sent.

## Rules for every message

- Tell the truth: new, one-person brokerage. Do not claim customers, savings percentages, "exclusive access" or supplier relationships you do not have yet.
- Always disclose: you are not the provider, and you may be paid a commission by the provider. If you charge the buyer a fee, state it in writing before they commit.
- Never promise a price or availability. Quotes are indicative until the provider confirms in writing.
- Mention free public compute when it fits the buyer better. It costs you a deal and earns trust.
- No firm quote or supplier introduction before the intake form (incl. KYC / end-use) is complete and screening is passed.
- Cold email to EU contacts: send individually to business addresses, make it relevant to their role, include who you are and an easy opt-out, and stop when asked. Rules differ by country — confirm with a lawyer (see questions in `/research/demand-side.md`).
- Under 130 words. One ask. Fill every `[bracket]`; delete anything not true on the day you send.

## 1. Cold intro — email (English)

**Subject:** GPU capacity for [Company] — a question

Hi [Name],

I saw that [Company] is [specific, true observation: e.g. training its own speech models / hiring ML engineers / just raised a seed round].

I run [Your LLC name], a new one-person GPU brokerage. I help AI teams in Europe find capacity — usually 8–64 GPUs for 1–12 months, often EU-hosted — by collecting and comparing offers from several providers.

To be clear about how I work: I am not the provider; your contract is with them. I may be paid a referral commission by the provider, and I will tell you if that applies to any offer I bring.

One question: how are you getting compute today, and is anything about it a problem (price, availability, location, commitment length)?

If it is not relevant, just say so and I will not write again.

[Name]
[Your LLC name] · [website] · [LinkedIn]
