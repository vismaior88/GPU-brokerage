# Business plan summary and 30/60/90-day plan
_Version 1 — 2026-11-09. Proposals in this file are recommendations awaiting the founder's decision (see `decisions/decision-log.md`)._

## One-page business plan

**What it is.** An independent GPU compute sourcing service. I help European and Western Balkans AI teams find, compare and contract EU-hosted GPU capacity, and I am paid a disclosed referral commission by the supplier.

**Customer.** Funded AI startups, AI agencies and research groups that need **8–128 GPUs for 3–12 months**, care where the data sits, and have no procurement person.

**Offer.**
- Free: a vetted shortlist of 2–3 suppliers, comparable quotes within 48 hours, help arranging test access.
- Paid (later, optional): a fixed-fee "sourcing sprint" with a written comparison of prices and contract terms.
- Always: written disclosure of any commission, and a clear statement that I am not the provider.

**Why a buyer would use me.** Not price. A verified EU/SEE supplier map, real vetting, contract-term comparison, a named person who answers in English or Serbian, and visible compliance. This advantage does not exist yet and has to be earned deal by deal.

**Why a supplier would pay me.** Mid-size, committed customers they would not have reached, already qualified and KYC-checked. Second-tier and regional EU suppliers with small sales teams are the realistic partners; the largest clouds are not.

**Revenue.** 5–10% of customer spend, usually for 6–12 months. One 32-GPU H100 deal at $3.00/hour is about $3,500–$5,600 a month. First-year target: 2–4 closed deals. No cash expected before month 4–6.

**Costs.** Under $2,000 in six months: US Form 5472 filing, one Montenegrin tax consultation, one US lawyer session if the budget allows, a domain and website. All tools on free tiers.

**Competition.** Well-funded marketplaces and brokers (SF Compute, Hydra Host, Andromeda, Ornn, BGC's compute desk, NVIDIA's own partner matching), most free to buyers. They focus on large or self-serve deals; mid-size EU/SEE buyers who want a person are the gap.

**Rules I will not break.** KYC and screening before any introduction. No customers linked to China, Russia or other refuse-list countries. No physical hardware. No holding buyer funds. No crypto. No futures. No workarounds.

**Main risks.**
1. Cash: months with no income on a small budget.
2. Tax: Montenegro is likely to tax the LLC's income; this must be settled before first revenue.
3. Compliance: one bad deal can bring personal penalties.
4. Market: when capacity is sold out, suppliers need brokers less.
5. Being bypassed: non-circumvention clauses are weak; written deal registration matters more.

**What would make me stop.** By day 90, fewer than 2 supplier agreements and fewer than 5 qualified buyer conversations; or by month 6, no deal in contract.
