# Red-flag list
_Version 1 (draft for lawyer review) — 2026-11-09_

**How to use:** check every deal against this list. **One unresolved flag: pause and ask in writing. Two: refuse.** Flags marked ⛔ are immediate refusals. Write down each flag and how it was resolved. The flags come from real enforcement cases and official guidance; see `research/enforcement-cases.md`, `research/export-controls.md` and `research/sanctions-and-screening.md`.

## Who the buyer is
- ⛔ On any restricted-party list, or 50%+ owned by someone who is
- ⛔ Located in, headquartered in, or ultimately owned from a refuse-list country (see `compliance-policy.md`, Rule 2)
- ⛔ Refuses to name owners, headquarters, end user or workload
- Company formed after October 2022, or only weeks before the deal
- No website, a thin website, or language versions that say different things
- Main business has nothing to do with AI (trading, real estate, logistics)
- Residential, virtual-office or freight-forwarder address
- Local "subsidiary" of an IT company from a refuse-list country
- Shares an address, director or phone number with a listed party
- Contact uses a free email address or a domain that does not match the company

## How the deal behaves
- ⛔ Asks me to hide the end user from the supplier, or to put a different name on paperwork
- ⛔ Asks whether the GPUs can be used from a restricted country, or mentions VPNs or proxies for that purpose
- ⛔ Someone tells me not to mention a country or a company
- The named end user never appears; an agent or "consultant" speaks for them
- Someone from an unrelated company is copied on emails
- Willing to pay well above market, or offers a large prepayment with little negotiation (reported in November 2026: a Chinese AI firm offering European GPU clouds 30–50% more upfront)
- Great urgency, very large first order, no technical questions
- Vague end use ("AI research", "internal projects") that stays vague when asked
- GPU count far beyond what the company's size and funding explain
- Users will log in from a country unrelated to the buyer
- Wants to resell or sub-let the capacity to unnamed others
- Asks me to sign or pass on certificates I cannot verify myself

## Payment
- ⛔ Wants to pay in crypto or cash
- ⛔ Payment offered by a third party unrelated to the buyer
- Payment from a bank in a country unrelated to the buyer, especially China or Hong Kong
- Asks me to receive the buyer's money and forward it to the supplier (I never do this)
- Requests to split invoices, change the invoiced entity late, or overpay and refund

## Supplier
- ⛔ Capacity located in a refuse-list country, or supplier owned from one
- ⛔ Supplier or its owners on a restricted-party list
- ⛔ Any request involving physical hardware: ship, store, relabel, re-invoice
- Cannot say how it obtained its GPUs
- Cannot or will not confirm that its customers are not headquartered in China
- Data centre cannot be located, or cannot show power and cooling for the claimed hardware
- Price far below market for scarce GPUs
- No test access before payment; demands a large prepayment to an unrelated account
- Hardware does not match the description on test access (model, memory, count, interconnect)
- Anonymous website, no named people, no company registration details
- "EU sovereign" or "data stays in the EU" claims with no written contractual commitment

## About me (self-check)
- I am tempted to skip a step because the deal is large or urgent
- I have stopped asking questions because I am afraid of the answer — this is exactly what the law calls willful blindness
- I am relying on someone's verbal assurance in place of a document

## When a flag appears
1. Stop work on the deal. Do not introduce the parties.
2. Ask the question in writing and keep the answer.
3. Verify the answer from an independent source.
4. If it is resolved, note how. If not, refuse politely and without explaining which check failed.
5. Never suggest how the buyer could change the deal to pass.
6. If I think a violation may already have happened, speak to a lawyer before doing anything else.
